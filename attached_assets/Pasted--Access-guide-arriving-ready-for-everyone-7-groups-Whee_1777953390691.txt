🛡️ Code of conduct
🫶
Respect everyone
All ages, all genders, all identities, all career stages. The first-year gets the same airtime as the staff engineer.
🎤
Share the mic
If you've spoken twice, wait until everyone who wants to has spoken once. This is a rule, not a vibe.
📷
No photos without consent
Teal wristband = no photo. Ask before posting anyone on social.
🚫
No self-promotion spam
Talks are for ideas, not pitch decks. Sponsors have their own slot.
⏸️
Safe-word: "pause"
Anyone can call "pause" to stop a session for a moment. No explanation needed.
🛡️
Report quietly
Any concern → DM any wing lead or safety officer. Handled privately, within 24 hours.
🤝 Community pacts · the six
6 lines
🌿
Leave the place greener than you found it.
Pick one thing up · even if it isn't yours. Bring one seed · even if you don't plant it.
🙌
Use first names · no titles.
No ‘sir’, no ‘ma'am’ · inside the event. Alumni are guests, not authority.
📴
Phones down during talks · up during ideas.
Silence mics during sessions · but capture notes and photos of saplings.
📸
Ask before you photograph anyone.
Consent first · green-lanyard means ‘yes, please’, red means ‘not today’.
👏
Cheer loudly for the first-timer.
Open mic, first talk, first poem · we hold the room for them.
🥡
Leftovers are shared · never thrown.
Any unclaimed food goes to the Ops wing bag · carried to the shelter that same night.
